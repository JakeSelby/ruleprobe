# Code of conduct

This applies to everyone taking part in this project: issues, Discussions, pull requests,
reviews, and any other channel the project uses.

## Expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here are about what a detector should count, and reasonable
  people differ.
- Critique the work, not the person. "This detector will fire on every heredoc body" is
  useful; "you clearly don't understand agents" is not.
- Keep it on topic and keep it concrete. An example beats an adjective.
- Say when you were wrong. This repository is built on the idea that an honest status is worth
  more than a polished one.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind, including on the basis of who someone
  is.
- Publishing someone else's private information, such as a home address or a private email,
  without their explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of a thread after being asked to stop.
- Any behaviour that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome contributing.

## Reporting

Report privately to the maintainer through the email address on the maintainer's GitHub
profile, or use GitHub's report-content links on the offending item. Reports are read within a
week and kept confidential.

## Enforcement

The maintainer decides what happens, in proportion to what happened: a private note, a public
warning, a temporary block, or a permanent block from the project. Decisions are final for
this project.
