## Goal

A stranger runs `ruleprobe report` with no flags and sees their own rules found, bound per sentence and measured inside 60 seconds, with the binder scored like a detector.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In: per-sentence binding (FR-36), the binder's corpus score on a synthetic zoo (FR-37), rule discovery with no `--rules` (FR-38), catalog patterns for the four unbound defaults. Out: the 200-section binding corpus (Epic 15), git-aware windows (Epic 14).

## Children

Proposed, unfiled until the epic starts: 8.1 binder spike, 8.2 binder score in `corpus`, 8.3 per-sentence binding, 8.4 catalog patterns for the defaults, 8.5 rule discovery (epics.md, Epic 8).

## Architecture slice

AD-18 (per-sentence binding under the under-count rule), amending AD-12's binding unit; AD-4; AD-6. `ruleprobe/rules.py`, `ruleprobe/detectors/catalog.py`, `ruleprobe/validity.py`.

## Sequencing

Housekeeping #121 and #124 lands first. 8.1, then 8.2, then 8.3; 8.4 and 8.5 follow 8.3 and can run in parallel.

## Exit criteria

Per-sentence binding recall is at least 0.70 on the zoo with zero false binds, and a fixture home shows a measured rule with no flags inside 60 seconds.
