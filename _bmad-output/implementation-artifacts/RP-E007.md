# RP-E007 — Epic 5: A third runtime

<!-- bmad-sync:begin -->
- **GitHub issue:** [#26](https://github.com/JakeSelby/ruleprobe/issues/26)
- **Primary parent:** None
- **State:** completed

The issue carries the summary, discussion and acceptance evidence; this file carries the design.

This work item was authored as part of the repository's committed BMad planning system.
<!-- bmad-sync:end -->

## Goal

A developer on a third runtime gets the same detectors, scored on labelled sessions from that
runtime. Done when `ruleprobe report --runtime <name>` reads the chosen runtime, and every applicable
detector scores at or above 0.9 on its labelled sessions in CI. Runtime-neutral becomes a fact
(maintainer decision 5).

## Scope and requirement coverage

- In: confirming the chosen runtime, Gemini CLI (PRD open question 1, decided by the maintainer on
  2026-09-23); a reader for it (FR-32: a third reader, Gemini CLI); labelled sessions from it with near-misses at the floor
  (FR-33: labelled third-runtime sessions in the corpus, at or above the floor).
- Out: a fourth runtime; a lossy tool mapping to gain hits (AD-3); new event kinds beyond what the
  reader needs (AD-2).

## Children

1. RP-SP003 (#54): choose the third runtime, Cursor or Gemini CLI. The maintainer chose Gemini CLI on
   2026-09-23; the spike confirms its format and file-tool mapping.
2. RP-S019 (#55): read the third runtime's transcripts into the event schema.
3. RP-T003 (#56): labelled third-runtime sessions with near-misses, at the floor.

## Architecture slice

- AD-1: Dependency direction (the new reader module takes its place in the graph).
- AD-2: The event schema is the one contract between readers and detectors.
- AD-3: One tool vocabulary across runtimes.
- AD-6: The labelled corpus is the validity contract, and the floor is 0.9.
- AD-10: How a reader is added.
- Components: `ruleprobe/readers/`, `ruleprobe/corpus/`, `README.md`.

## Sequencing

- RP-SP003 has no dependency and can start now. RP-S019 depends on RP-SP003. RP-T003 depends on
  RP-S019 and story 3.5.
- The epic can run in parallel with Epic 4.

## Exit criteria

1. The runtime choice and its file-tool mapping are recorded in RP-SP003.
2. `--runtime <name>` and `auto` read the runtime's sessions, with AD-10's error behaviour.
3. The corpus holds at least one labelled session from each runtime read, with near-misses.
4. Every applicable detector, catalog entries included, is at or above 0.9 on those sessions in CI.
5. The README names the runtime and what its transcripts do not record.
