# RP-E002 — Future: a judge seam in ruleprobe and a separate, provider-neutral judge library

The issue carries the summary, discussion and acceptance evidence; this file carries the design.

This work item was authored as part of the repository's committed BMad planning system.
<!-- bmad-sync:end -->

## Goal

Rules no transcript shape can decide become measurable by a model judge, without any model
entering `ruleprobe report`, and every judge is scored on ruleprobe's labelled corpus exactly as a
detector is. Observable completion: a judge from outside ruleprobe emits verdicts into ruleprobe's
row shape, and `ruleprobe corpus` prints its precision and recall beside the detectors'.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In scope, in order:

1. A verdict-source seam in ruleprobe, proposed for 0.3: a verdict record marked as judged with
   provider, model id and pack version, and judge scoring on the corpus. It extends PRD FR-19
   (the report as data) and FR-23 (corpus score and floor), and must keep NFR-3 (deterministic
   report) and NFR-7 (no model in measurement) true for `report`.
2. A separate, provider-neutral judge library, future and unscheduled, carved out of
   agent-harness's decision layer once the extraction signals below hold.
3. Drafting a declarative detector from a rule's prose, moved here from ruleprobe by the maintainer
   on 2026-09-23 because it needs a model (formerly ruleprobe FR-34, RP-E009 and RP-S020). It lives
   with the judge library, and its output is data a person reviews and commits to ruleprobe.

Out of scope: any model call, client, credential or vendor adapter inside ruleprobe; publishing
figures for a provider whose terms bar it.

## Children

None yet. The seam's stories are written when 0.3 is planned; the library's when its extraction
signals hold. Related harness spike: which harness capabilities stand alone as packages.

## Architecture slice

- ruleprobe spine AD-8 (the package envelope) and AD-11 (compliance beside hits) bound the seam:
  judged verdicts travel beside deterministic ones, never replace them.
- agent-harness spine AD-15 (decision providers are subordinate) governs the judge until it is
  extracted, and AD-13 keeps the dependency direction: the judge depends on ruleprobe.

## Sequencing

The 0.2 versioned contract (Epic 1 in `epics.md`) lands first, because
the verdict record extends the row schema it versions. The seam follows in 0.3. The library is
extracted last, when all three signals hold: outside demand for dark-rule measurement with one's
own model; a judge that needs nothing from the harness beyond the seam; a validity figure the
provider's terms allow to be published.

## Exit criteria

- The seam is specified in the PRD and the spine and delivered in a ruleprobe release.
- A judge outside ruleprobe is scored by `ruleprobe corpus` at the floor.
- `ruleprobe report` still calls no model, verified by the envelope test from Epic 1.
