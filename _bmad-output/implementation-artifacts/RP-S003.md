## Story

As the maintainer, I want a product brief that states what ruleprobe is, who it serves first,
what it will not become, and what 0.2 must do, so that the PRD and spine inherit one position
instead of re-deciding it.

## Context and value

ruleprobe's intent existed only inside agent-harness's corpus, where it is one feature of the
harness. The field research found two neighbours that already measure rule compliance on Claude
Code, so the package needed a position of its own. The maintainer set it on 2026-09-23: the
measurement engine for agent rules, not a doctor, narrow and library-first.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the research artifact and the maintainer's decisions, **when** `bmad-product-brief`
   runs, **then** `brief.md`, `addendum.md` and a memlog exist under `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/briefs/brief-ruleprobe-2026-09-23/`.
2. **Given** the brief, **when** it names alternatives, **then** it credits claude-md-doctor and
   RuleReceipt by name and makes no claim to be first or only.
3. **Given** the brief, **when** it describes 0.1, **then** it says plainly what 0.1 does not do
   for a stranger's own rules.
4. **Given** a fresh-context validation, **when** it reports findings, **then** each is applied
   through the skill's update intent and logged, or recorded as not applied with a reason.
5. **Given** the configured `doc_standards`, **when** the structure and prose lenses run, **then**
   their findings are applied without changing what the brief claims.

## Design

### Approach

Headless Fast path from the research artifact, the README, `AGENTS.md` and the agent-harness brief,
with the maintainer's nine decisions supplied as binding rules. Then a validation in a fresh
context, an update applying its findings, and the configured polish lenses in a third context.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **Position: engine, not doctor.** The maintainer's decision. Prescriptions, report cards, cause
  triage, blocking hooks, hosted features and any model call in `report` are out.
- **The claude-md-doctor comparison rests on its own source.** The first draft carried an
  assumption that its matchers are authored per run. It was verified from the project's skill
  file and `backtest.py` and restated as a fact with its citation, with no claim that two runs
  differ.
- **Success numbers are proposals.** Every target and the 2027-03-23 window are tagged
  `[ASSUMPTION]` for the maintainer to set.

### Do not implement

- The PRD, spine or stories; each has its own issue.
- Moving the success-criteria detail into the addendum to reach two pages; the polish pass left it
  because every line carries a claim.
