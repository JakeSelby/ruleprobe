## Goal

Reports pool across a team without session keys, the formats are published, and the corpus is proven to catch a broken detector.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In: judge receipts (FR-35) and the dark-rule slice (FR-47) under #21, `merge` (FR-48), JSON Schemas (FR-49), mutation testing (FR-50), the binding corpus (FR-51). Out: the judge itself, a separate package (#115).

## Children

Proposed, unfiled until the epic starts: 15.1 judge receipts, 15.2 dark-rule slice, 15.3 merge, 15.4 JSON Schemas, 15.5 mutation testing, 15.6 binding corpus (epics.md, Epic 15). #21 (RP-E002) covers 15.1 and 15.2; #115 (RP-T007) beside.

## Architecture slice

AD-15, AD-17, AD-18, AD-6, AD-7, AD-8 (no model).

## Sequencing

15.1 then 15.2; 15.3 then 15.4; 15.5 and 15.6 in parallel.

## Exit criteria

Each corpus catches at least 80% of mutants, binding precision is at least 0.95 on at least 200 sections, and a merge of three reports equals their union.
