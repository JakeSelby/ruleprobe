## Goal

Anyone can judge a sample of a detector's hits and near-misses locally and hand back a card holding counts and no content; the cards become the shipped field figure.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In: detector version hashes (FR-39), `ruleprobe audit` (FR-40), validity cards and `ruleprobe/validity/field.json` (FR-41), retaking the four pre-fix figures and publishing agreement. Out: judge receipts (Epic 15).

## Children

Proposed, unfiled until the epic starts: 10.1 version hash, 10.2 audit, 10.3 cards and the field figure, 10.4 retake figures and publish kappa (epics.md, Epic 10).

## Architecture slice

AD-15 (counts-only cards), AD-16 (version hashing), AD-13 (redaction), AD-8 (writes only under a user-named directory).

## Sequencing

10.1, 10.2, 10.3 in order; 10.4 after 10.3 and Epic 9's #110 and #111.

## Exit criteria

A test proves no transcript string reaches a card, and a human has re-judged at least 30 agent verdicts with kappa published.
