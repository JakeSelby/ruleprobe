## Story

As the maintainer, I want the 0.2 scope broken into epics and work items, each filed as an issue
with a BMad ID and a story file that carries its design, so that 0.2 can be built one item at a
time with `bmad-build` and no builder has to re-read the whole PRD to start.

## Context and value

The PRD and spine fix what 0.2 does and the invariants it keeps. Without a breakdown, a builder
would pick scope ad hoc, and the decisions that gate the contract (the schema version, the
declared API, the versioning policy) would be made mid-build by whoever reached them first.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the PRD and spine, **when** `bmad-create-epics-and-stories` runs, **then**
   `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/epics.md` maps every in-scope FR to at least one item, with
   no forward dependency inside an epic.
2. **Given** an open question that blocks a story, **when** the epics are written, **then** it
   becomes a decision or spike item sequenced before the story it gates.
3. **Given** the epics, **when** the readiness gate runs in a fresh context, **then** its findings
   are applied or recorded as open questions, and spine or PRD amendments land in the same pull
   request.
4. **Given** each item, **when** it is filed, **then** it carries a `type::*` label, a BMad ID, its
   epic as parent, and the v0.2.0 milestone unless it is proposed.
5. **Given** each filed item, **when** its story file is read, **then** its required sections are
   filled from the corpus and `audit --delivery` reports no findings.

## Design

### Approach

Headless run of the skill's four steps, with the PRD and spine named explicitly because they sit
in dated folders. Then the readiness gate in a fresh context, one update applying its findings,
filing through `scripts/bmad_issue_sync.py new` from a script that parses `epics.md`, and five
parallel passes filling the story files by epic.

### Decisions and alternatives

- **The versioned contract goes first.** Every later item writes rows and entries against it, and
  agent-harness pins the result.
- **Bug #19 is placed, not duplicated.** It became story 1.7 and a sub-issue of Epic 1; its
  acceptance criteria were widened to all of AD-4 after the readiness gate found the original
  "Done when" covered only part of it.
- **Proposed work stays off the milestone.** Epic 7 (drafting from prose) and the field-precision
  spike carry no milestone until the maintainer confirms them.
- **The judge seam is not a 0.2 item.** It is FR-35, proposed for 0.3, and the future epic
  RP-E002 (#21) tracks it and the separate judge library.

### Do not implement

- Any 0.2 code. Each item is built from its own story file in its own pull request.
- The judge seam or library.
