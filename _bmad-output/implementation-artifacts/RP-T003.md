## Goal

Make "runtime-neutral" a measured claim: every detector that applies to the third runtime, catalog
entries included, is scored on labelled sessions from that runtime and meets the 0.9 floor in CI.
SM-6 targets exactly this, with near-misses as its counter to easy sessions.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the corpus, **when** listed, **then** it holds at least one labelled session from each
   runtime read.
2. **Given** each detector that applies to the third runtime, catalog entries included, **when** CI
   scores it on those sessions, **then** it is at or above 0.9.
3. **Given** the third runtime's sessions, **when** reviewed, **then** they carry near-misses as well
   as positives.

## Tasks

- [ ] Author synthetic native-runtime sessions for the third runtime (AC: 1)
- [ ] Label positives and near-misses per applicable detector in `labels.yaml` (AC: 2, 3)
- [ ] Run `ruleprobe corpus --floor 0.9` and fix or report any detector under it (AC: 2)
