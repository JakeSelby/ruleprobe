## Goal

Every default detector has field evidence behind it, or it leaves the defaults.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In: the NFR-10 floor (point estimate at least 0.90, Wilson lower bound at least 0.80, at least 20 judged hits from 2 developers), the FR-8 amendment, and the fixes #110, #111, #113 and #114. Out: capturing the evidence itself (Epic 10).

## Children

#114 (RP-D012), #110 (RP-B009), #111 (RP-B010), #113 (RP-B012), and proposed story 9.5, the floor test (epics.md, Epic 9).

## Architecture slice

NFR-10; AD-15 (the field figure), AD-17 (the Wilson bound), AD-4. `ruleprobe/detectors/`, `README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`.

## Sequencing

#114 before #110; #111 and #113 in parallel. 9.5 closes the epic after Epic 10's field figure (10.3) and Epic 11's bounds (11.1).

## Exit criteria

Each default is at least 0.90 with a Wilson lower bound of at least 0.80 on at least 20 judged hits from 2 developers, or it has left the defaults under a Breaking changelog heading.
