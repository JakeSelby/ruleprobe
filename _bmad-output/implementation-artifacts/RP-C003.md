## Goal

Fix every finding of the pre-publication review before ruleprobe's first public release. A
fresh-context adversarial review of `main` at `e022b3b`, run on 2026-09-22, recorded 25 findings,
most severe first. Nine of them (items 1, 3 to 8, 11 and 20) blocked publication because they made
the instrument report a number that is not the behaviour: a false hit, a silently dropped
denominator, an unreachable grouping, or swallowed reader errors. A false hit breaks the package's
stated contract to under-count rather than over-count.

The review also recorded what it did not find: no personal paths or names beyond `LICENSE`, no real
transcript fragments (`docs/example-session.jsonl` is synthetic), correct `pyproject.toml` fields and
a present `__version__`.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** each of the 25 findings, **when** the suite runs, **then** a regression test in
   `tests/test_findings.py`, named after the finding's number and built from the reviewer's own
   input, passes. Nothing is deferred or skipped.
2. **Given** a detector with a `gate:` block and `--stance dim=variant` on the command line, **when**
   `ruleprobe report` runs, **then** the gated detector fires and `--by stance` groups by stance
   (findings 1 and 2).
3. **Given** a `cat <<\EOF` heredoc, or a plain `<<` heredoc whose terminator is indented, **when**
   the command is parsed, **then** the body lines are not parsed as commands and produce no false
   `unfiltered-find` hit (findings 3 and 4).
4. **Given** text that does not tokenize, such as an unbalanced quote, **when** it is parsed, **then**
   it is `unparsed` rather than invisible to every matcher (finding 5).
5. **Given** one detector that raises, **when** a session is measured, **then** only that detector's
   denominator loses the session, and a reader that produces nothing for a transcript is counted
   and reported (findings 6, 7, 9 and 20).
6. **Given** `--json`, **when** the report prints, **then** it carries the table's denominators,
   folds and notes and exits as the text report does; `--since 2024` is refused (findings 11
   and 24).
7. **Given** the labelled corpus, **when** `python3 -m ruleprobe corpus --floor 0.9` runs, **then**
   every detector meets the floor, with the two heredoc shapes labelled as near-misses.

## Dev notes

### Bound decisions and requirements

No PRD or architecture spine exists yet, so no FR or AD applies. The work is bound by the package
contract in `AGENTS.md`: deterministic output, and under-count rather than over-count.

### Decisions recorded in the pull request

- **Finding 16 is a refusal, not a conversion.** `yes` could have been read as `True` and `010` as
  `8`; refusing both with a line number matches the rest of `declarative.py`, because a file two
  readers disagree about is worse than one that does not load. Two tests that relied on the old
  reading now quote their value.
- **Finding 6 leaves the legacy `rules_error` spelling dropping the row whole.** It names no
  detector, so there is nobody to charge the loss to; the plural spelling `measure()` writes is the
  one that is now per-detector.
- **Finding 23 changed the claim, not the world.** The shipped six detectors use ten matchers
  because that is what their observables need.
- **`--json` output shape changed** from a bare list of rows to the report object with the rows
  under `rows`. Pre-1.0 and recorded in `CHANGELOG.md`.
