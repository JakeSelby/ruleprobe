## Story

As a contributor changing the binder,
I want `ruleprobe corpus` to score rule binding like a detector,
so that a binding regression fails CI.

## Context and value

Story 8.1 (RP-SP004, #137) scored today's section binder on a synthetic rules zoo of 65 sections
and 84 labels: recall 0.44 (18/41), precision 0.95 (18/19), one false bind. The false bind is `t12`,
"Run the tests before finishing. Docs-only changes are exempt.", because "exempt" is not an
exception word. Nothing in CI scores binding, so story 8.3's per-sentence binder, and any catalog
or word-list change, could add false binds unseen. This story makes the zoo package data and a
gate, so 8.3 lands measured (FR-37, AD-18). RP-SP004's decision moves the 0.70 recall bar from the
binder to Epic 8 as a whole, so this story reports recall and holds it with a ratchet, not a bar.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** the zoo shipped under `ruleprobe/corpus/`, **when** `ruleprobe corpus` runs, **then**
   it prints a binder section with binding precision and recall per catalog entry and in total.
2. **Given** a false bind in the zoo, **when** `corpus --floor 0.9` runs, **then** it exits
   non-zero, whatever the floor: binding precision on the zoo must be 1.00.
3. **Given** `--json`, **when** it runs, **then** the binder figures appear under `binding`,
   beside `detectors`.
4. **Given** binding recall under the recorded floor, **when** `corpus` runs, **then** it exits
   non-zero; **given** recall above the floor rounded down, **when** the suite runs, **then** a
   test says to raise the floor.
5. **Given** "Docs-only changes are exempt." after a rule, **when** the section binds, **then** it
   is unmeasured, and the shipped binder makes no false bind on the zoo.

## Design

### Approach

- Move `tests/fixtures/rules-zoo/zoo.json` to `ruleprobe/corpus/rules-zoo.json` byte for byte
  (sha256 `0a22f9b2…a0325a`, as frozen in RP-SP004) and add `corpus/*.json` to package data.
- `ruleprobe/validity.py` gains `load_zoo`, `score_binding`, `binding_failures`, `binding_table`
  and `binding_as_dict`, and a `Binding` tally of one `Score` per catalog entry. Each item is
  written as `## heading` plus its lines and bound through `rules._units` and `rules._bind`, as
  RP-SP004's section binder was. Per section: expected is the catalog ids its labels name (folded
  through the shipped fold map), bound is what `_bind` measured; true, false bind and miss follow.
  Labels naming a detector with no catalog pattern are counted apart as `outside_catalog` (4).
- `BINDING_RECALL_FLOOR = 0.43`: today's 18/41 rounded down. A test fails when recall rises past
  the next hundredth, so the story that raises recall raises the floor.
- `cmd_corpus` prints the binder section under the detector table, writes `binding` into the
  JSON, and fails on any `binding_failures` line, independently of `--floor`.
- `rules._EXCEPTION` gains `exempt(?:ed|ions?)?`.

### Decisions and alternatives

1. **False binds gate at zero, not at the floor.** One false bind in 19 is 0.95, over 0.9, so the
   detector floor cannot catch it; FR-37 says a false bind fails as a detector under the floor
   does. Rejected: a binder precision floor of 0.9.
2. **Recall is a ratchet at 0.43, not a 0.9 or 0.70 bar.** RP-SP004's decision moved the recall
   bar to Epic 8; gating recall at 0.9 would fail today's binder. Rejected: no recall guard at all,
   which lets a word-list change silently unbind true rules.
3. **The zoo lives beside `labels.yaml` and is read from the corpus directory in use.** A corpus
   of your own with no zoo scores no binding and says so; the shipped corpus without its zoo is a
   `CorpusError`, so a packaging slip fails the installed-wheel `corpus` step in CI rather than
   passing it. Rejected: always scoring the shipped zoo, which ignores `--corpus`.
4. **The zoo is unchanged, `about` text included,** so its frozen hash still holds. Its `about`
   names RP-SP004 and story 8.4, public planning ids in this repository.
5. **A rate with nothing to divide prints `-` and is null in JSON,** rather than `Score`'s 0.00,
   which would read as a wrong bind for an entry that bound nothing.
6. **No new name joins the declared API.** `scores_as_dict` keeps its shape; `cmd_corpus` adds
   the `binding` key.

### Do not implement

- Per-sentence binding, negated markers, or next-sentence exception scope (story 8.3).
- Catalog patterns for the four uncatalogued defaults (story 8.4), or any pattern widening.
- Any label change in the zoo.
