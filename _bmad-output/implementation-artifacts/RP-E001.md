## Goal

ruleprobe has a public planning corpus of its own under `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/`: a
dated field research artifact, a product brief, a PRD, an architecture spine, and the epics and
stories for 0.2. Each is written for the package as the product, cites public sources, passed a
fresh-context review, and merged through its own pull request. The epic is done when all five
children are closed and `docs/bmad-governance.md`'s amendment clause has documents to amend.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In scope: the five artifacts named in the children, authored with their BMad skills from the
shared checkout on the Fast path, each reviewed by the matching skill's validate intent in a
fresh context before merge. Out of scope: any change under `ruleprobe/`; the 0.2 implementation
itself, which the last child files as its own issues; changes to the agent-harness corpus, which
belong to that repository.

No PRD exists before this epic, so no FR ids bind it. The PRD child defines them; the spine child
binds them to ADs; the epics child covers them with stories.

## Children

1. RP-S002, #11: field research as a deep-recon artifact (competitive type).
2. RP-S003, #12: product brief.
3. RP-S004, #13: PRD.
4. RP-S005, #14: architecture spine.
5. RP-S006, #15: epics and stories for 0.2.

## Architecture slice

None of the package's components change. The slice is the planning corpus itself:
`_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/{research,briefs,prds,architecture-spines}/` and the story files
under `_bmad-output/implementation-artifacts/`. The spine child records the first ADs; until it
merges, `AGENTS.md`'s package contract is the only written invariant.

## Sequencing

Strictly sequential: each child is an input to the next. The research artifact feeds the brief's
alternatives; the brief's decisions are inherited by the PRD; the PRD drives the spine; the
spine and PRD drive the epics. Nothing runs in parallel except the fresh-context review of one
artifact while the next is drafted, and a review finding that changes a decision re-opens the
earlier artifact through its skill's update intent before the later one merges.

## Exit criteria

- Five merged pull requests, one per child, each closing its issue, with
  `scripts/bmad_issue_sync.py audit --delivery N` clean for each.
- Every artifact carries a memlog and cites only public sources.
- The PRD's FR ids and the spine's AD ids are the ones the 0.2 stories bind.
- `docs/bmad-governance.md` needs no text change: its conditional amendment clause now applies.
