## Goal

A developer asks whether a rule change changed behaviour and gets detected, inconclusive or confounded, with an honest interval.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In: `ruleprobe compare` over windows (`--split-at DATE`, opt-in `git log`) and groups (stance, model, runtime), Newcombe intervals, minimum detectable effect, controls and confound warnings (FR-46). Out: a judge, hosting, prescriptions.

## Children

Proposed, unfiled until the epic starts: 14.1 Newcombe and minimum detectable effect, 14.2 compare, 14.3 confound warnings and git-aware windows (epics.md, Epic 14).

## Architecture slice

AD-17 (statistics), AD-16 (refusal on a hash mismatch), AD-15 (saved rows). `ruleprobe/stats.py`, `ruleprobe/report.py`, `ruleprobe/cli.py`.

## Sequencing

After Epic 11. 14.1, 14.2, 14.3 in order.

## Exit criteria

Fixtures with an effect, with none and with a model shift read detected, inconclusive and confounded, and a changed detector hash refuses.
