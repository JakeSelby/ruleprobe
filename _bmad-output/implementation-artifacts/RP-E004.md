## Goal

For a rule that asks for something to be done, a developer sees how many opportunities there were and how many were followed, beside hits per session (maintainer decision 3). The epic is done when `report` and `report_data` show `opportunities`, `followed` and `undecided` for every `order` detector and every `absent` detector with `scope: turn`, and hits per session still print for every detector.

## Scope and requirement coverage

In scope (planned, v0.2.0):

- FR-21 Compliance per opportunity: opportunities and followed beside hits (RP-S011, RP-S012, RP-S013).
- FR-22 Minimum opportunities: no rate below the minimum (RP-D004, RP-S013).
- FR-6 Detector and Registry (touched): `Detector` gains the keyword-only `opportunities` attribute (RP-D005, RP-S011).
- FR-9 A failing detector costs only itself (touched): a raising `opportunities` costs only its own figures (RP-S012).
- FR-17 Hits per session (touched): the `promote?` wording is settled in the break (RP-D006, RP-S013).

Out of scope: compliance by position in the session (PRD §8 Out, Q5; confirmed or reopened by RP-D007). A trigger key for `absent` is an AD-7 vocabulary change the spine defers. `run()` and its return do not change.

## Children

6. RP-S012 (#42): feat(report): rows carry compliance for detectors that define opportunities.
7. RP-S013 (#43): feat(report): compliance per opportunity for order and absent detectors.

## Architecture slice

- AD-11 Opportunity and compliance travel beside hits, never inside them: the attribute, the triple shape, the compile rules for `order` and `absent`, the row's `compliance` map and the minimum.
- AD-4 Detectors under-count rather than over-count: an undecided opportunity is counted as `undecided`, never as not followed.
- AD-9 The contract is versioned, once, in 0.2.0: the fold function folds `compliance` as it folds `rules`; the contract test covers the new attribute.
- AD-7 The declarative format is a closed YAML subset: no new matcher key.
- AD-8 The package envelope: `report` stays deterministic, with byte-identical `--json`.

Components: `ruleprobe/registry.py`, `ruleprobe/matchers.py`, `ruleprobe/report.py`, `ruleprobe/cli.py`, `README.md`.

## Sequencing

- The four decisions have no dependencies and can run in parallel. RP-D007 must close before the 0.2.0 release story (6.2).
- RP-S011 depends on RP-D005 and on Epic 1 stories 1.7 (#19, the undecided rule) and 1.8 (the contract test).
- RP-S012 depends on RP-S011 and on Epic 1 story 1.5 (schema version on rows).
- RP-S013 depends on RP-D004, RP-D006 and RP-S012, and on the fold function from Epic 1 story 1.6.

## Exit criteria

1. Every child is closed.
2. A fixture session with N opportunities of which M are followed yields `opportunities: N` and `followed: M` in `report_data`, and the table prints the same numbers beside hits per session.
3. For every compiled detector on the corpus, hits = opportunities - followed for `absent` and hits = followed for `order`.
4. Below the minimum opportunity count, counts print and no rate does, in the table and in `report_data`.
5. The gate in `AGENTS.md` is green, on the 3.9 floor, with no new dependency.
