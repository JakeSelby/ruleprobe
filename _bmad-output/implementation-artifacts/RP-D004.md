## Context

FR-22 says no compliance figure is shown below a minimum opportunity count, and that the minimum is a parameter of `report` and `report_data`. PRD Q7 asks whether 20 is the right value; the PRD's working default mirrors `min_sessions=20`, which today gates `unobserved` and `promote?` in `report_data`. AD-11 applies the minimum to `opportunities` and names 20 as the working default. The question: what is the default, and does it apply per rule or per printed group under `--by repo` and `--by stance`? A rate over three opportunities invites action on noise.

## Options

1. **20, per printed group.** Mirrors `min_sessions`, which the report already applies to each printed block. For: one mental model for both floors, and a `--by repo` block with few opportunities shows no rate. Against: a small repository may never show a rate.
2. **20, per rule across all groups.** For: a rule measured widely shows a rate in every group. Against: a group's rate can then rest on a handful of its own opportunities, which is what FR-22 prevents.
3. **A different default (lower or higher).** For: opportunities are per turn or per `first` match, so they may accrue faster than sessions and a higher floor could cost little. Against: no measurement in the corpus supports a specific other number yet.

## Decision

Decided 2026-09-23 by the maintainer: the minimum is 20 opportunities, applied per printed group.
It mirrors `min_sessions`, which the report already applies to each printed block, so both floors
share one mental model.

## Consequences

- RP-S013 applies `min_opportunities` wherever it applies `min_sessions`; the parameter default is 20.
- Tests cover a group under the floor, which shows no rate.
- A small repository may never show a rate; the minimum stays a parameter, so a caller can lower it.
