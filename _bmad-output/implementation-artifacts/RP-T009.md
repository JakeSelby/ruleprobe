## Design

- **Numbering.** Epic 7 (#28, drafting a detector) is withdrawn but keeps its number, so the new epics
  run 8 to 15, and their story ids (8.1 onward) do not collide with Story 7.1 and 7.2. New FRs continue
  from FR-36, and new PRD sections 13 and 14 go after the assumptions index so no existing section
  reference moves.
- **Milestones and issues.** v0.3.0: Epics 8 to 13 (steps 1 to 6), #128 to #133 (RP-E010 to RP-E015).
  v0.4.0: Epics 14 and 15 (steps 7 and 8), #134 and #135 (RP-E016 and RP-E017).
- **Decisions carried in, all approved 2026-09-25.** History past Claude Code's 30-day deletion is an
  opt-in `ruleprobe snapshot DIR` with `report --rows` and a `cleanupPeriodDays` warning (PRD Q14).
  Rule discovery reads today's rule text, stated in the coverage block (Q15). The default-set floor is
  a point estimate of at least 0.90 and a Wilson lower bound of at least 0.80 on at least 20 judged
  hits from 2 developers, and `secret-in-write` leaves the defaults until #110 lands (Q16). 0.3 is the
  launch (Q17). Outside validity cards are accepted per source and excludable with a stated reason
  (Q18). At least a fifth of every sample is human-judged, with agreement published (Q19). Canary
  sessions come after 0.4 as a `scripts/` tool (Q20).
- **Amended rules.** The PRD's "no team aggregation" non-goal now admits a local, content-free
  `merge`; NFR-5 and AD-8 let `audit` and `snapshot` write under a directory the user names; FR-8's
  six-detector promise gives way to NFR-10; AD-12 points at AD-18 for the binding unit.
- **Existing issues.** #110, #111, #113 and #114 sit under Epic 9, #112 under Epic 11, #27 alongside
  Epic 13, and #21 over Epic 15's judge receipts and dark-rule slice. #115 sits beside Epic 15;
  housekeeping #121 and #124 lands before Epic 8.
- **Alternatives rejected.** Keeping history only through `--json` output the user saves, which leaves
  most first comparisons empty; and discovering rules only for sessions newer than each rule file's
  last change, which empties most first runs. Both are in the PRD addendum.
